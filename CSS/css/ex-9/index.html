Community Bulletin

The City Council has announced several new initiatives aimed at improving community engagement and public services. Residents are encouraged to participate in upcoming events, including clean-up drives, cultural festivals, and educational workshops.

A new community center is also being developed to provide spaces for learning, recreation, and social activities. The project is expected to be completed by the end of the year and will serve people of all age groups.

Local authorities have also introduced environmental awareness programs to promote sustainable practices. Citizens can join tree plantation campaigns and recycling initiatives organized across different neighborhoods.

For more information about community programs and events, residents can visit the official city portal or contact the local council office.
